Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

RWA Today ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how the website at rwa.today handles information. In short: we do not collect personal data.

Information we collect

RWA Today is a static, informational website. We do not collect, store, or process personal information. We do not require registration, do not operate accounts, and do not run forms, sign-ups, or payment systems.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics or visitor-tracking tools on this site. No tracking identifiers are placed on your device by us.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files through a third-party hosting provider. Like most web hosts, the provider may automatically record standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and time of access) in server logs for security and operational purposes. This processing is carried out by the hosting provider under its own policies, not by RWA Today, and we do not access or use this data to identify individuals.

Third-party links and data

Pages may link to third-party websites (for example, data sources and project websites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Market data displayed on the site is sourced from third parties such as DeFiLlama; please refer to their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves (for example, if analytics are added in the future). Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
