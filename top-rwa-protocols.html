Methodology

This leaderboard ranks tokenized real-world asset (RWA) protocols by total value locked (TVL), using the most recent daily snapshot (UTC).

The raw data — including TVL, protocol name, symbol, chains and logo — comes from DeFiLlama's open API. Any statistics and calculations shown on the site are produced by RWA Today from that data.

Only protocols with at least $1M in TVL are listed. Figures change daily and may be revised as the source data updates.
