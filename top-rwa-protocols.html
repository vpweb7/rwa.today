Methodology

This leaderboard ranks tokenized real-world asset (RWA) protocols by total value locked (TVL). Underlying TVL figures come from DeFiLlama's open API and reflect each protocol's tracked on-chain value at the most recent daily snapshot (UTC).

Only protocols with at least $1M in tracked TVL are included, to keep the list to assets with meaningful, verifiable on-chain value.

The Share column shows each protocol's TVL as a percentage of the combined TVL of all listed ($1M+) protocols, based on the latest snapshot.

Rankings and shares are computed by RWA Today from daily snapshots; only the raw TVL is sourced from DeFiLlama. Figures change daily and may be revised as source data updates.
